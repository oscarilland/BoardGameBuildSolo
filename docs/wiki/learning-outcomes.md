# Learning outcomes

What players should be able to do after a game, from the SUB1a report (section 1.4), and where the game teaches each one. The [game loop diagram](../raw/net-zero-belfast-handover/design/game_loop.png) tags each stage with its outcomes.

| ID | Players will be able to... | Taught by |
|---|---|---|
| LO1 | State NI's legal targets: a 48% cut by 2030 and net zero by 2050 | Interim targets in 2030 and 2040 ([Climate Meter](rules/climate-meter-and-disasters.md)); the 2030 target marker on the meter bar ([FR-15](requirements/fr-15-always-visible-status.md)); quiz cards Q01, Q02 |
| LO2 | Identify NI's main emission sources: agriculture, transport, buildings, energy | Square names (gas plant, airport, motorway); [Climate Cards](rules/climate-cards.md) Q03, C02, C03, C05, E09 |
| LO3 | Explain why short-term profit from emissions harms everyone in the long run | Polluting income vs the shared meter; money worthless if net zero is missed ([scoring](rules/scoring-and-end-of-game.md)); each player's share of emissions in the end-of-game report ([FR-16](requirements/fr-16-end-of-game-report.md)) |
| LO4 | Distinguish mitigation (Green Projects) from adaptation (Resilience) | Green Projects lower the meter; Resilience only cuts your own disaster costs ([board and squares](rules/board-and-squares.md)) |
| LO5 | Explain why delaying action makes climate impacts more costly | Disaster chance and cost grow with the danger band; interim penalties; card C08 "wait and cut later" |

## In the software

The SUB1a report (section 1.6) also counts sustainability in the software itself: offline with no servers ([NFR-08](requirements/nfr-08-privacy-and-security.md)), idle CPU while players think ([NFR-11](requirements/nfr-11-energy-efficiency.md)), runs on existing PCs ([NFR-09](requirements/nfr-09-portability.md)), and content in data files so it can be updated as NI's figures change ([NFR-07](requirements/nfr-07-data-driven-balance.md)).

# Source - Rules v4

## Provenance

- **Files:** [rules_v4.md](../../raw/net-zero-belfast-handover/docs/rules_v4.md) and the original [Net_Zero_Belfast_Rules_v4.docx](../../raw/net-zero-belfast-handover/docs/Net_Zero_Belfast_Rules_v4.docx). The Markdown file is a conversion of the Word file; on ingest the two were compared paragraph by paragraph and match, apart from the Word file's title line "Net Zero Belfast - Revised Rules (Draft v4)".
- **Dated:** 11 November 2026, by Oscar. Drafted with Claude and agreed by the team.
- **Authority:** the player-facing rules. [HANDOVER.md](handover.md) wins where they differ.

## What it covers

Overview, resources and scoring, characters, board and squares, Resilience, turns and rounds, bankruptcy, Climate Meter and disasters, interim targets, end of game, learning features, balance testing results, the changes from v3, and open questions.

## What it contributed

- Player-facing wording for every page in `rules/`.
- The balance results and the v3 → v4 change table, on [balance and design history](../rules/balance-and-history.md).
- Its six open questions, merged into [open questions](../rules/open-questions.md).

## Limitations and caveats

- "Round 12 is 2048; when the year reaches 2050 the game ends" - consistent with HANDOVER's "after round 12 (year reaches 2050)".
- Says "a loop of 36 squares through about 6 Belfast districts". The handover clarifies that it's a 30-square loop plus a 6-square cut-through.
- The turn order lists "convert" as a landing action (step 2) and again as step 3 ("if you did not convert in step 2"). HANDOVER clarifies the limit is one conversion per turn in total.
- "Write the Climate Card content" is still listed as open here, but the 30-card deck now exists in [the data files](data-files.md).
